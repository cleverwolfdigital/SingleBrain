## Notes on how these were assembled

**The Eldoria Odyssey** was supplied as three separate book titles — *The Ordinary Odyssey*,
*Cosmic Cafeteria*, and *The Echoes of Destiny*. They are not separate books. They are chapters
1, 2, and 4 of a single continuous story, along with *The Cosmic Collaboration* (3) and
*The Enigma Within* (5). The text itself settles it: Chapter 1 closes by promising "a tale that
would unfold over 15 chapters," each chapter opens where the last one ended, and the same three
protagonists and guide carry through all five. They are filed as one project accordingly — say the
word if you want them split anyway.

**Science and Progress** has a first chapter and a twenty-fifth chapter and nothing between them.
The Ch. 25 fragment is short but it fixes the ending, which makes the missing middle unusually
tractable to outline.

**Life Reset: Button** had no files at all. The folder, the chapter stubs, and the recorded
positioning copy were all created here on 2026-07-28. Nothing was recovered because there was
nothing to recover — details in that project's master file.

## Conventions

- Chapter files are `NN_Title.md`, numbered to sort.
- Every chapter file carries a status line and a link back to its Google Doc source where one exists.
- Imported prose is **verbatim** — typos, grammar slips, and mid-sentence endings are preserved.
  Corrections are logged as open questions in each project's `Notes/`, never applied silently.
- Google Docs remain the version of record for previously-written material until you decide to
  switch. Editing both will diverge them.
