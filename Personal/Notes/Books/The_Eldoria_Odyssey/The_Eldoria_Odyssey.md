## Premise

Three friends — **Alexius**, **Maya**, and **Ethan** — work dead-end cubicle jobs in the quiet town of
Eldoria and escape into shared imagination on their lunch breaks. A cosmic anomaly reveals that the
things they invent are *literally* reshaping the universe. The **Aeon Guardian**, a shape-shifting
cosmic entity, recruits them to restore the balance between space and time.

The engine of the story: **creativity has consequences.** Every world they dream up becomes real
somewhere, and they are accountable for it.

## Where the next chapter has to start

Ch. 5 ends with the trio suspended in a vortex, pulled by the Unseen Observer toward a **convergence
point where past, present, and future choices collide**. Three threads are live and unresolved:

1. Each of the three has an **unresolved internal conflict** deliberately left open.
2. The **Aeon Guardian's hidden agenda** has been foreshadowed twice but never paid off.
3. The **Unseen Observer** has been introduced but not explained.

Ch. 6 is the natural place to collide at least two of these.
