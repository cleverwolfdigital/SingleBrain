"Hi, this is my wife Corinda and my two boys" a middle aged man said while introducing us.

"A pleasure to meet you both. If you two boys are as smart as your father the world would in be twice as much trouble." the man chuckled although the sound of his voice had a hint of seriousness.

My dad continued, "Why don't you boys go ahead and play in the lab while me and Mr.xxxxx talk business" my dad would often say his name but, I could never really remembered it clearly.

"Edgar.." the man would say. "The lab is not playground for little boys. Do be careful and whatever you do, don't break anything."

We were always sent to the lab to "keep busy" I would read fantasy books about wizard, dragons and the dungeons that kept them.

In the back of my mind I knew that Wizards, Mages and Sorcerers were all different. I didn't know it at the time, but this came to be important later in my life.

My brother, Josiah, on the other hand LOVED science. Every chance that he had, he would go to the lab and test all types of scientific theories.

At what temperature would copper melt? What is the strongest organic materials? What withstood huge amounts of pressure?

The best way to describe my brother...Well, we definitely looked the same but I'm much more handsome, of course.

Josiah was always quiet and reserved, he took calculated risk when it came to jumping into anything.

"Everything needed to be solved with logic", he would say and for me that meant boring.

Don't get me wrong I love my brother but when you're 12 years old and always working on the latest gadget or tinkering with an electronic device things can get...again...boring.

For what felt like a million years to me, finally came to an end when our Dad came into the lab. My dad handed us two items which looked like cool new toys.

"Each of you, keep these safe." the moment it touches my hand a flash comes over me.

"Nothing in nature is controllable, but the mind controls all. The world is only as we see it. Pro...t..." I hear my dad say while holding something in his hand. I can't quite make out what it is though.

In today's society, my dad is a mixture of science and magic. We call them Wizards. They cast spells, are inventors, philosophers and scientist. My dad is at the highest level you can achieve in Wizardry.

A wizard must train regularly to upkeep his skills and there are 7 areas of color that a wizard can master.

Green - Healing and Protection

Red - Fire and PK Strength (Telekinesis)

Blue - Water and Ice

Yellow - Wind and Earth

Black - Spatial Magic

White - Light

Grey - Light and Spatial Magic but to a lesser degree

I've been studying this for years in hoping that someday, I too could sit on the counsel with my dad.

Josiah has no such goals but he does take after our dad when it comes to an interest in science. At times, I feel there is nothing my brother can't fix or even create in technology. At the age of 12, that's pretty amazing. I'm a slow learner and on top of that, I am slow to learn any type of magic.

My dad has a Master's Degree in Electrical Engineering from the University of Manoa. Although he did get his certifications in 4 areas of color in Magic, he then met my mom Corinda while studying a 5th area of color.

So far, my dad has the most certifications with 4 and a degree in science, so in this day and age it's a pretty amazing feat. I know that a lot of his colleagues expect a lot from us, but at the end of the day, we're proud he's our dad and of the works he's accomplished.

Our dad created the gray magic category and is known as the Keeper of the Grey. All the keepers meet twice a month to discuss new findings, new discoveries and of course anything that would be considered a new magic category.

People talk about us all the time and they coined us, "Children of the Gray." I guess short for Children of the Gray Keeper. Unfortunately, I am unable to perform any gray magic, which my dad always tells me, "in time it will come, focus on what you're reading and it should come to pass."

I come to and I realize that the lab is in shambles, my brother is missing.

"What is going on?" I say to myself. I look around and got a grasp of the situation.

It looks like the lab was attacked. I look above and there is a green bubble around us.

Green Magic.

My dad must have cast a protection spell around us. If that's the case, where is dad? And where is my brother?

Staff around us are in a panic and I come to and the green bubble dissipates.

Everything is hazy.

I'm still holding what my dad gave me and I know he gave one to my brother as well. I go searching for my mom and see that she is in the same green bubble.

"Thank goodness" I say to myself. She was protected too.

That's when I see it. The horror. The beginning of what will be named in history, "APO Incident."
