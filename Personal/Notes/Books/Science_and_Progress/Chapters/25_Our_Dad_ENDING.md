No one would've guess that I was the one to create the explosion that cause the beginning of the Apocalypse and what makes matters worse, was the fact that all this time as I've been studying magic to be the best that I could be. I'm the one who ended becoming the Dark Wizard.

The relic is shattered, there is no holding back. It is time to wipe out the potential that my dad held me back.

Now the only person who can stop us and save the world - is my brother.

---

## Why this fragment matters more than its length

It reveals three things the rest of the manuscript has to earn:

1. **The narrator is the villain.** The unnamed "I" of Chapter 1 — the one who loves fantasy, is
   slow at magic, and desperately wants to sit on the council with his dad — becomes the Dark Wizard.
2. **The narrator caused the APO Incident**, the catastrophe he appears to be a *victim* of in Ch. 1.
   Chapter 1's amnesiac haze ("Everything is hazy", "I come to") is doing double duty as an
   unreliable-narrator device.
3. **Josiah is the real protagonist** — the quiet, logical brother is who ends up saving the world.

Everything between chapters 1 and 25 is the story of how the sympathetic kid becomes the thing that
ends the world. That is the spine.
