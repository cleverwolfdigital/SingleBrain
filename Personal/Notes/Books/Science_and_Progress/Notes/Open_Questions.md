# Open Questions — Science and Progress

Found while importing the two existing fragments on 2026-07-28.

---

## Structural

### 1. There is no middle ⚠️ the whole project
Chapter 1 and Chapter 25 exist. Chapters 2–24 do not. The ending already fixes the destination
(narrator → Dark Wizard, Josiah → hero), so the outline has a known target — which makes this very
outlinable, but it does have to happen before more prose.

### 2. Chapter 1 stops mid-sentence
It ends on "What we didn't know was that" — no period. Whatever completes that sentence sets the
hook for the entire book.

### 3. The narrator and the father are both unnamed
The mother (Corinda), the brother (Josiah), and a visitor (Edgar) all have names. The two most
important characters don't. In a first-person narrative the narrator's name being withheld can be a
deliberate device — but if so, Ch. 25's reveal needs to land on it.

---

## Setup planted, payoff missing

### 4. "Wizards, Mages and Sorcerers were all different"
Ch. 1: *"I didn't know it at the time, but this came to be important later in my life."* An explicit
promise to the reader. Nothing in the existing text delivers on it. Likely relevant to how the
narrator becomes something other than a Wizard.

### 5. The two objects
The father hands each boy an item, says "keep these safe," and a flash of vision follows. In Ch. 25:
*"The relic is shattered."* Presumably the same object — but there were two, one per twin. What are
they, and what happens to Josiah's?

### 6. The father's cut-off words
*"Nothing in nature is controllable, but the mind controls all. The world is only as we see it. Pro...t..."*
The unfinished word is probably "Protect" — worth confirming that's intended, since it's the
father's last line in the book so far.

### 7. What is the "APO Incident"?
Named as historically significant. The acronym is never expanded. Ch. 25 calls the same event "the
beginning of the Apocalypse" — so APO may simply be short for Apocalypse, or an organization/site.

### 8. Grey magic and the narrator
The narrator *cannot* perform grey magic; his father says "in time it will come." His father also,
per Ch. 25, "held me back." Is the Dark Wizard turn powered by grey magic finally arriving, or by
him abandoning it for something else?

---

## Craft

### 9. Chapter 1 is an unedited first draft
Representative issues, all in the source text:
- "the world would in be twice as much trouble" — stray "in"
- "I could never really remembered it clearly" — tense
- "The lab is not playground for little boys" — missing article
- "he took calculated risk" — number agreement
- "sit on the counsel with my dad" — should be *council*
- "the strongest organic materials" — number agreement
- Dialogue punctuation is inconsistent throughout (commas vs. periods before attribution)

Worth one clean line-editing pass — but **after** the outline, not before. No point polishing prose
that may get restructured.

### 10. Two chapters share the title "Our Dad"
Almost certainly intentional (opening and closing bookend). Confirm, and consider whether Ch. 25
wants a distinguishing subtitle for the manuscript's table of contents.
